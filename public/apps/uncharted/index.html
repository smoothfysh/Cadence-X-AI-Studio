On-Device Privacy Commitment

Uncharted: Travel Log does not collect, transmit, sell, or store your personal travel history on any developer-operated server. All visited countries, statistics, and generated summary images stay on your Android device unless you explicitly choose to share or back them up.

1. Introduction

Uncharted: Travel Log ("the App", package ID com.cadencex.uncharted.app) is developed and published by Cadence-X ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App on Android.

We do not operate any backend servers for Uncharted: Travel Log. Your travel log is stored locally on your device.

2. Information Processed on Your Device

To provide the App’s features, Uncharted: Travel Log processes the following information locally on your device:

  • Visited Countries & Travel Log Data: The list of countries you mark as visited, along with continent statistics and display preferences, stored in a private on-device SQLite (Room) database.
  • User-Initiated Backup & Import Files: When you choose Backup / Restore or share a Google Maps link, CSV, JSON, or Takeout archive with the App, the App reads or writes only the file or text you explicitly select.
  • Exported Summary Images: When you tap Save JPEG to Gallery or Share Story, the App renders a JPEG image locally on your device and either saves it to your device gallery (Pictures/Travelog) or hands it to the Android share sheet for the app you choose.
  • Optional Google Sign-In / Maps Import: If you optionally use Google Sign-In or Maps import features, authentication is handled via Google’s official Play Services / OAuth flow solely on your device to import your saved places. We never see your Google password and never receive your data on any external Cadence-X server.

3. Data Sharing, Advertising & Analytics

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with third parties.

  • The App contains no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • The App contains no third-party analytics, crash-tracking, or behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • No human at Cadence-X has access to your visited country list or backup files.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Clear Inside the App: You can remove individual countries or use the Clear All Visited option in the App menu at any time.
  • Clear App Storage or Uninstall: Clearing app storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Uncharted → Storage → Clear Storage) or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local App data from your device.
  • Manual Backups & Saved Images: Any JSON backups or JPEG images you saved to your own storage remain under your control and can be deleted anytime using your Files or Gallery app.

5. Children’s Privacy

Uncharted: Travel Log is a general-audience travel utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or legal requirements, we will publish the updated policy on this page and update the "Last Updated" date above.
