HOW IT WORKS

Set it up once. Let it work quietly in the background.

Coincide Drive is designed for simplicity. You don’t need to manage individual file uploads or worry about overwriting edits made on another device and every subfolder inside your chosen folder is synced automatically.

Step 01

Connect Google Drive

Sign in securely using Google’s official sign-in flow. You can even connect different Google accounts for different folder pairs; Coincide Drive never sees your password.

Step 02

Link Your Folders

Choose a folder in your Google Drive and a folder on your phone. All files and nested subfolders are kept in 2-way sync so apps like Obsidian, Markor, or PDF readers can edit them locally anytime.

Step 03

Control & Monitor Live

Pause or resume individual folders with a single toggle, watch live file progress (e.g., Syncing 12/40), inspect detailed sync history logs, and set rules like "Only on Wi-Fi."

SMART DIFFERENTIAL SYNC ENGINE

Only Changed Files Are Transferred

Coincide Drive never re-uploads or re-downloads your entire folder tree on every schedule. Instead, it performs a fast, lightweight check across all subfolders and transfers only the exact files that changed.

Fast checks.
Zero wasted data.
🔍

1. Recursive Metadata Scan

At each sync interval, the app scans your linked folder and all nested subfolders, checking modification times and MD5 checksums without downloading file contents.

🗃

2. Local Snapshot Comparison

It compares the current phone and Drive states against a local cache stored privately on your phone to pinpoint exactly what was added, edited, or deleted.

⚡

3. Delta-Only Transfers

If you have 1,000 files and edit just 1 note, only that single note is uploaded or downloaded. If nothing changed, 0 bytes of file content are transferred.

🛡

4. "Keep Both" Safety

If a file was edited on both your phone and computer before a sync occurred, neither version is lost. Both copies are saved side-by-side with a timestamp.

1. Introduction & Architecture

Coincide Drive ("the App") is developed and published by Cadence-X ("we", "us", or "our"). Coincide Drive is a client-side Android application that synchronizes files and subfolders between up to 5 user-selected folders in the user's Google Drive account(s) and corresponding user-selected directories on the user's Android device.

We do not operate any backend servers for Coincide Drive. All authentication, file scanning, unlock code verification, and file transfers occur directly on your Android device and between your device and Google’s official servers over encrypted connections.

2. Information Accessed and Processed

To provide two-way file synchronization, Coincide Drive accesses and processes the following categories of data strictly on your Android device:

  • Google Account Profile Information: When you sign in with Google, the App receives your email address, display name, and profile photo URL via Google Play Services. This information is displayed in the App's interface so you know which account is linked to each folder pair and is stored locally in your device's private application database/preferences.
  • Google Drive Files and Metadata: With your explicit authorization (via the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope), the App lists folders so you can select sync targets, queries storage quota information, and reads, uploads, downloads, updates, or deletes files within the specific folder pair(s) you configure.
  • Local Device Files: Using Android’s Storage Access Framework (SAF), the App reads and writes files exclusively inside the local phone folder(s) you explicitly grant permission to during setup.
  • Local Sync History, File Index & Unlock Status: The App maintains a local SQLite database (via Android Room) and DataStore preferences inside the App's private sandboxed storage on your phone. This stores relative file paths, modification timestamps, MD5 checksums, recent sync history/error logs, and your offline 5-folder unlock status (CADX-YYYY-YYYY).

3. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used exclusively to provide the user-facing functionality of the App:

  • Authenticating your session with the Google Drive API for each active folder pair.
  • Comparing file modification times and checksums between your selected Google Drive folder(s) and your selected phone folder(s).
  • Uploading new or updated local files to your Google Drive folder(s), and downloading new or updated Google Drive files to your phone folder(s).
  • Resolving edit conflicts losslessly by preserving both copies when a file is edited in both locations simultaneously.
  • Displaying live file sync counts, recent sync history, and detailed error diagnostics on the App's home screen.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

  • On-Device Storage Only: All configuration settings, sync pair mappings, and baseline file metadata are stored locally on your Android device within the operating system's private application directory.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between Coincide Drive and Google APIs (Google Sign-In and Google Drive REST API v3) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.
  • No Developer Access: Because Cadence-X does not operate intermediary servers or cloud databases for Coincide Drive, we have no technical means to view, access, or recover your files, filenames, or account tokens.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or Google Drive data with any third parties.

  • The App contains no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • The App contains no third-party behavioral analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No human at Cadence-X ever reads or accesses your Google Drive files.

6. Data Retention, Deletion & Revoking Access

You retain full control over your data and permissions at all times:

  • Unlinking Individual Folders or Deleting Local App Data: You can pause or unlink any synced folder pair directly from the App's home screen at any time. Clearing the App's storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Coincide Drive → Storage → Clear Storage) or uninstalling the App permanently and immediately deletes all local sync logs, cached file hashes, and account preferences. Unlinking a folder or uninstalling the App does not delete your actual files in Google Drive or in your phone's local folders.
  • Signing Out: You can sign out or switch Google accounts directly within Step 1 of the App at any time.
  • Revoking Google Account Permissions: You can revoke Coincide Drive’s access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Account Security & Permissions page.

7. Children’s Privacy

Coincide Drive is a productivity and file-management utility not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
