# Apps & Experiments — Cadence-X Art Direction

*Small tools. Thoughtfully made.*

I build focused web tools to help with real work, one project at a time.

---

## Design Philosophy

The Cadence-X `/apps` page is art-directed to feel like an **independent software studio portfolio** — human, intentional, understated, and distinctive. It deliberately avoids the visual cliches of generic AI-generated landing pages and SaaS templates:

- **No uniform card grids**: Each product or experiment has its own tailored composition.
- **No decorative clutter**: Elimination of badge spam, arbitrary gradients, neon glows, and emoji substitutes for design.
- **Authentic visual previews**: Real product interfaces (Kanban board, Gantt timeline, radial timer dial) replace fabricated mockups.

## Products & Experiments

### 1. Roadmap (Featured Product)
- **Label**: `FEATURED PRODUCT`
- **Tagline**: *"Plan, track, and ship. A lightweight roadmap tool for product, engineering, and design teams."*
- **Positioning**: Built for product clarity from two decades of hands-on delivery experience. Bypasses enterprise project management bloat to focus strictly on clear priorities, realistic horizons, and shared alignment.
- **Key Features**: Instant local boards, no-login cloud sync, real-time viewer updates, visual dependency clash tracking, and dual Kanban / Gantt timeline views.
- **Destinations**: 
  - Live Tool: [https://roadmap.cadence-x.com/](https://roadmap.cadence-x.com/)
  - Capabilities Tour: Native interactive modal (`#roadmap-modal`)

### 2. Otis Play (Experiment / 02)
- **Label**: `EXPERIMENT / 02`
- **Tagline**: *"A little corner of playable web content made for young kids — simple, colorful, tactile, and immediately fun."*
- **Aesthetic**: Warm, sunny, tactile pastel sandbox contrasting with the structured Roadmap tool.
- **Pillars**: Zero tracking, no accounts, instant one-click touch play.
- **Destination**: `/otisplay/`

### 3. Timer App (Experiment / 03)
- **Label**: `EXPERIMENT / 03`
- **Tagline**: *"A minimal timer for focus sessions, workouts, presentation rehearsals, or simply anything on the fly."*
- **Aesthetic**: Dark, focused precision interface featuring the glowing cyan radial countdown ring and interval presets.
- **Pillars**: Radial, Brutalist, and Editorial skins; interval presets; zero accounts.
- **Destination**: [https://timer.cadence-x.com/](https://timer.cadence-x.com/)
