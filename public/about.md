I'm a seasoned product person by trade, engineer by training, and the reason a kids’ games site and a product manager's roadmap tool share a homepage. Turns out 20+ years of shipping software leaves you with some odd side projects.

## The Quick TL;DR Version About Me

I’ve spent 20+ years bouncing between software design for mobile phones, e-commerce, martech and now digital real-estate — and I’ve worn most of the hats along the way: software engineer, product planner, product manager, strategist.

That mix has taught me how to navigate messy, ambiguous problems — the kind that show up when a company changes hands or a market shifts overnight — and still keep teams shipping. I care a lot more about getting the thing delivered than getting credit for it; execution is what actually moves the needle.

If I had to summarise myself, I’d say:

**I’m Passionately curious** | **I strangely enjoy babysitting chaos** | **Obsessed about solving the problems my customers have** | **Nerd at heart** | **Product Strategist** | **Advisor and Consultant**

If there’s a throughline, it’s this: I’m at my best translating technical innovation into something people can actually feel the benefit of. That’s as true for a roadmap tool a PM opens every morning as it is for a game simple enough for a five-year-old to figure out alone — which is more or less why Cadence–X exists. No grand unifying theory, just things I wanted to build, living under one roof.
