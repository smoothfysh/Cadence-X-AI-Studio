Product Capabilities Tour

What Can Roadmap Do?

A comprehensive breakdown of features designed for agile clarity, zero bloat, and total data ownership.

🔄 Cloud & Local Flexibility

Instant Local Boards

Create unlimited, independent roadmaps entirely in your browser with zero network latency or mandatory cloud storage.

No-Login Cloud Sync

Sync boards to the cloud and share them using secure edit keys and read-only URLs—no user accounts required.

Live View Updates

Read-only cloud roadmaps update in real-time for viewers whenever the owner publishes changes.

Password Protection

Lock cloud edit links with optional passwords for an extra layer of security.

📊 Visual Timelines & Dependencies

Smart Dependency Tracking

Draw "waits on" or "blocks" arrows between cards. The system automatically highlights date clashes in orange if a blocker is scheduled to finish after its dependent is due.

Gantt Milestones

Track key dates with diamond milestones attached directly to your Gantt timeline bars, which automatically fill in as dates pass.

Auto-Generated Tags

Type prefixes like V1: or Phase 2: in your titles—the app automatically extracts them and converts them into color-coded badge pills.

Fuzzy Due Dates

Translate specific dates into flexible "Early," "Mid," or "Late" month bands for high-level timeline planning.

⚡ Workspace Organization

Rolling History Dividers

Completed items automatically fold out of the way into neatly separated past-quarter and past-year groups.

Focus Controls

Collapse all lanes with one click to hide empty rows and focus strictly on active teams and current quarters.

Clutter-Free Interface

Card indicators are strictly capped at three markers (dependencies, links, milestones) to ensure the board remains highly readable at scale.

Native Drag-and-Drop

Seamlessly reorder tasks and manage timelines with smooth, responsive drag-and-drop mechanics.

💾 Data Portability & Integrations

Smart Link Badges

Paste external URLs, and the board automatically detects the provider (like Jira or Confluence) and applies the correct icon.

Lossless CSV Handling

Import and export robust, quote-aware CSVs that fully preserve complex descriptions, paragraph breaks, and item dependency chains.

Named Scopes & JSON Backups

Publish read-only previews directly via JSON files or download secure, local JSON backups at any time.
